HOW THE DRIVING INDEX WORKS
1
Pick a target and mark a 30-yard-wide fairway around it — 15 yards either side of your line. Then mark the small-miss boundary 15 yards outside each fairway edge. Anything wider is a big miss. The corridor never changes.
2
Push five tees into the turf beside you in that same order. Hit, look, then drop one ball behind the matching tee. Twenty drives, twenty balls.
3
A big miss costs two fairways, so the piles at the edges are the ones that move your number. Count them into the app and your carry distance turns the total into a handicap.
